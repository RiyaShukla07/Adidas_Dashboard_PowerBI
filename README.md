# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Adidas sales
performance across products, retailers, regions, states, and time. The
dashboard converts sales data into actionable business insights for
monitoring performance and supporting strategic decision-making.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Requirements

The business requires an interactive dashboard that enables stakeholders
to:

-   Monitor overall **sales, profit, units sold, average selling price,
    and profit margin**.
-   Analyze **monthly sales trends** to identify seasonal patterns.
-   Compare sales performance across **regions and states**.
-   Evaluate the contribution of different **retailers and product
    categories**.
-   Enable users to filter data by **Region and Invoice Date**.
-   Identify **top-performing products and retailers** to support
    strategic planning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  KPI                                 Description
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Total Sales**                     Overall revenue generated from
                                      Adidas sales.

  **Total Profit**                    Total profit earned from sales.

  **Total Units Sold**                Total number of units sold.

  **Average Price per Unit**          Average selling price per unit.

  **Average Profit Margin (%)**       Percentage of sales revenue
                                      retained as profit.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive KPI Cards** – Quickly monitor Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Units Sold, Average Price per Unit, and Profit Margin.
- **Monthly Sales Trend Analysis** – Track month-by-month sales performance and identify seasonal patterns.
- **Geographical Sales Analysis** – Compare sales performance across U.S. states using an interactive map.
- **Region-wise Sales Analysis** – Understand the contribution of each sales region to overall revenue.
- **Product Performance Analysis** – Compare product categories and identify top-selling products.
- **Retailer Performance Analysis** – Evaluate sales contribution from major retailers and identify top-performing channels.
- **Interactive Sales Channel Filters** – Analyze performance across In-store, Online, and Outlet channels.
- **Year-wise Analysis** – Compare sales performance across different years.
- **Month-wise Filtering** – Select individual months to perform focused analysis.
- **Interactive Cross-filtering** – Selecting a visual element dynamically updates related dashboard visuals.
- **Data-driven Decision Support** – Provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability to support strategic planning.
- **Executive-friendly Dashboard** – Uses KPI cards, charts, maps, and intuitive navigation for quick interpretation.

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend

A monthly trend chart shows how sales change throughout the year. It
helps stakeholders identify high-performing and low-performing months
and understand seasonal sales patterns.

### 2. State and Sales

The U.S. map visual compares sales performance across states, making it
easier to identify geographic markets with stronger sales contribution.

### 3. Region-wise Sales

The donut chart displays the contribution of different regions to total
sales and helps compare regional performance.

### 4. Product-wise Sales

The product comparison visual ranks major product categories by sales.
It helps identify products generating the highest revenue.

### 5. Retailer-wise Sales

The retailer analysis ranks retailers based on their sales contribution
and helps identify the strongest distribution channels.

### 6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive controls for analyzing different
periods and sales channels, including:

-   Month
-   In-store
-   Online
-   Outlet
-   Year

These controls allow stakeholders to dynamically explore the dashboard
and compare different sales scenarios.

## 🎨 Dashboard Design

The dashboard follows a structured layout:

**Top section** - KPI cards for quick performance monitoring - Sales
channel and year selection

**Middle section** - Monthly sales trend - Geographic sales analysis

**Bottom section** - Region-wise sales - Product-wise sales -
Retailer-wise sales

This layout enables users to move from **overall business performance →
trends → geographic analysis → product and retailer performance**.
